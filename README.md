## 👋 About us

We are a bioinformatics research group at the **[Malawi–Liverpool–Wellcome Research Programme](https://www.mlw.mw/)** (MLW) in Blantyre, Malawi.

Our work focuses on using genome sequencing and bioinformatics to better understand bacterial and viral pathogens, especially **how antimicrobial resistance develops and spreads**. Along the way, we build open, reproducible tools that we use in our own research and share with the wider community.

> Everything we build here is open. If our tools are useful in your work, we'd love to hear from you.

---

## 🔬 Research focus

|      | **Research area**        | **What we do**                                               |
| ---- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 🧫    | **Bacterial genomics**   | Study bacterial genomes to understand how bacteria change, spread, and cause disease. |
| 🦠    | **Viral genomics**       | Use genome sequencing to study viruses and how they evolve.  |
| 💊    | **AMR surveillance**     | Monitor antimicrobial resistance genes and how they spread in different populations. |
| 🌳    | **Phylogenetics**        | Build evolutionary trees to understand how pathogens are related and spread. |
| ⚙️    | **Workflow engineering** | Build open, reproducible analysis pipelines that make genomic data easier to process and analyse. |
